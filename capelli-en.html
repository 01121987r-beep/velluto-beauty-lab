Color art

Our specialization.

Color is not simply dye. It is a reading of the hair, the light and the face. At Velluto every coloring technique begins with an analysis of the hair and complexion, to create a tailored result rather than a standard effect.

  • Air Touch

    A contemporary technique born in Russia that uses a jet of air to separate shorter, finer hair from longer strands, applying color only to selected lengths. The blend is natural, the grow-out is soft and touch-ups may be needed only every 4 to 6 months.

  • Balayage

    The freehand color-painting technique, especially flattering on wavy and curly hair. The colorist works by hand on selected sections to create dimensional light tailored to the base tone and desired chromatic result.

  • Shatush

    A lightening technique that recreates the effect of the sun on the hair, working through the lengths with a soft, seamless transition. Ideal for those who want brightness without the sharp contrast of traditional highlights.

  • All techniques for the perfect blonde

    Classic highlights, Degradé, total blonde color, tone-on-tone Balayage and Air Touch: at Velluto the goal is always the same — a blonde that lasts, grows out beautifully and respects the hair. Every technique is chosen according to the hair's condition, the starting base and the desired result.

Hair care treatments

Protecting the hair is part of the final result.

Color and heat styling challenge the structure of the hair. At Velluto every restorative treatment is chosen according to the real condition of the hair, never offered by default.

  • Olaplex

    The best-known treatment for rebuilding the hair's disulfide bonds. It can be added to any color service to restore strength, softness and shine from the very first application.

  • Hair SPA treatment

    An intensive in-salon hydration and nourishment protocol with a mask selected for the specific hair type. Ideal for dry, dull or chemically treated hair.

  • Specific scalp SPA treatment

    A targeted treatment for себorrhea, dryness, dandruff or sensitivity. It includes scalp analysis, a specific exfoliation and a soothing serum.

  • Scalp peeling and detox mud

    Purifying treatments that remove product build-up and rebalance the scalp microbiome, ideal as a reset before a color or restorative program.
