About us

Velluto Beauty Lab. Rome, Via dell'Amba Aradam.

A beauty institute founded on technical expertise and personal attention. No excess, no vague promises. Only selected treatments, internationally respected brands and trained professionals dedicated to delivering visible results.

Every appointment begins with listening. The protocol is chosen according to the skin, the moment and the real objective. Results are built over time, and we are here to guide that process step by step.

Our values

At Velluto we work with brands that are not found everywhere: Mediplorer and Dr. Medion from the Japanese dermatological tradition, Thalion from Breton thalassotherapy, and Endospheres Evolution from Italian technology. A selection that reflects a clear position: the very best, without compromise.

  • Selection — Brands and technologies selected with rigorous clinical and professional criteria.
  • Method — Every treatment follows a defined protocol, adapted to the individual.
  • Continuity — Wellbeing is built over time. We are here to guide it.
  • Availability — Open every day of the week, because self-care does not follow only the working calendar.
  • Parking — Directly beneath the salon, so you can reach Velluto without worries.
