Brands and technologies

Professional protocols chosen to treat the skin with method, precision and progressive results.

Mediplorer

Japanese topical carboxytherapy

Mediplorer is the historic brand of Medion Research Laboratories, founded in 1997 by the Japanese dermatologist Dr. Masato Hiki. It is considered a reference in modern topical carboxytherapy and developed the first internationally patented CO2 gel mask.

The release of CO2 on the skin surface encourages the flow of oxygen-rich blood toward the treated area. The skin appears brighter, firmer and more even, with a regenerative action that builds session after session.

At Velluto we use the professional Mediplorer line, with CO2 concentrations up to 40% higher than consumer products and reserved for selected institutes.

Dr. Medion

Advanced Japanese cosmetology technology

Dr. Medion is the clinical brand from the same parent company as Mediplorer, developed for advanced professional applications. Its protocols address specific needs such as anti-ageing, couperose, adult acne and hyperpigmentation.

High-concentration Japanese actives are applied according to precise and codified sequences. Velluto Beauty Lab is among the very first institutes in Italy to introduce both Japanese houses into its treatment menu.

Thalion

Thalassotherapy from Brittany

Thalion is a leading European brand in professional marine cosmetology. Founded in Brittany in 1994, it works with algae harvested sustainably from the Iroise Sea and transformed into patented active ingredients.

Thalion protocols bring minerals, trace elements and algal acids to the skin through a professional, controlled approach already present in thalassotherapy centres and luxury spas in more than 50 countries.

At Velluto, Thalion treatments are offered for the face through the HydraSPA line and for the body within wellbeing and thalassotherapy rituals.

Microcurrents

Korean device for a toned, relaxed face

A latest-generation technology that works on facial muscles with low-frequency electrical impulses, similar to the body's natural bioelectrical signal.

The result is a more toned face, redefined contours and a more rested expression from the first sessions. It is ideal as anti-age maintenance and as preparation for special occasions.
